description: Upload video to YouTube with auto-generated metadata
---

Execute the skill at `~/.claude/plugins/bgm-creator/skills/bgm-creation` or `~/.claude/plugins/cache/masayan-uni-plugins/bgm-creator/skills/bgm-creation` and select "YouTubeに動画をアップロードしたい" to upload a video with bilingual metadata (English/Japanese), tags, and thumbnail.
